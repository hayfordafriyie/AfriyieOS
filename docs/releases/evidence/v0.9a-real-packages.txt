libafpkg against packages dpkg itself built
===========================================

The fixtures in build/fixtures are generated (see tools/pkgsynth.py for
why). These are not: they are built by dpkg-deb on this machine, and they
are what decides whether the reader works on a real distribution's
archive or only on data we wrote ourselves.

Three compressions, because dpkg offers all three and they are three
different formats rather than three settings of one.

WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES
  A .deb built by dpkg with gzip compression is read in full: name,
  version, architecture, dependencies, the multi-line description, and
  every file with its mode. That needed the complete DEFLATE
  implementation — stored, fixed-Huffman and dynamic-Huffman blocks —
  because dpkg's output uses all of them.

WHAT REMAINS
  Zstandard and xz. Both are refused by name rather than misread, which
  is why the failures above say exactly what is missing. xz is a
  different algorithm entirely; zstd is a different family. Neither is a
  variation of what is implemented.
